Today, the connection is missing.

  • Students and alumni have no direct, structured way to connect with each other.

  • Finding the right person for career guidance takes too much searching.

  • Job and internship opportunities don't reach students in time.

  • Alumni experience and knowledge stays under-used.

  • College events and opportunities are scattered, not centralised.

Setu closes that gap.

  • One shared platform where every student and alumnus builds a profile.

  • Alumni post jobs and internships; students apply in a click.

  • Students request mentorship directly from alumni in their target field.

  • Events and opportunities live in one organised, searchable place.

  • Admins oversee the whole network — users, postings, and activity.
